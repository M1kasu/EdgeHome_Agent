## **一、赛事背景 | Challenge Background**

随着大语言模型、多模态感知、智能体 Agent、AIoT  及空间智能技术的快速发展，家庭空间正在从“设备互联”走向“主动理解、智能决策与自主执行”的新阶段。传统智能家居更多依赖用户发出明确指令，而新一代智能空间系统需要能够理解家庭成员的真实需求，结合环境状态、用户习惯、设备能力和场景任务，主动完成更自然、更高效、更个性化的空间服务。

SpaceMind 作为面向智能空间的 Agentic  Architecture，聚焦真实记忆、自学习、主动智能和多设备协同能力，致力于让家庭空间从“被动响应”升级为“主动服务”。依托优秀的空间交互终端、AI 智慧决策能力、双路由交互架构及丰富的智能硬件生态，SpaceMind 可支持开发者围绕家庭生活中的真实场景，探索更具实用价值和创新性的 AI  Agent 应用。

本次赛题围绕“家庭应用场景 AI Agent”方向，鼓励开发者、创业团队、高校团队及生态伙伴基于  SpaceMind 理念，设计并实现面向家庭空间的智能体应用，探索 AI Agent  在居家控制、家庭服务、老人关怀、儿童陪伴、能源管理、安全守护、娱乐体验、健康生活等方向的创新应用，推动智能空间从单点控制走向全场景智能服务。

With the rapid development of large language models, multimodal sensing, AI  Agents, AIoT, and spatial intelligence technologies, home spaces are  evolving from "device connectivity" to a new stage of "active  understanding, intelligent decision-making, and autonomous execution."  Traditional smart homes often rely on explicit user commands, while  next-generation intelligent spaces are expected to understand real  household needs and proactively deliver more natural, efficient, and  personalized services based on environmental status, user habits, device capabilities, and scenario tasks.

As an Agentic Architecture for  intelligent spaces, SpaceMind focuses on real memory, self-learning,  proactive intelligence, and multi-device collaboration. It aims to  upgrade home spaces from "passive response" to "proactive service."  Supported by advanced spatial interaction terminals, AI-powered  decision-making, dual-route interaction architecture, and a rich smart  hardware ecosystem, SpaceMind enables developers to explore practical  and innovative AI Agent applications for real home scenarios.

This challenge focuses on "AI Agents for home application scenarios."  Developers, startup teams, university teams, and ecosystem partners are  encouraged to design and implement intelligent Agent applications based  on the SpaceMind concept, covering areas such as home control, family  services, elderly care, child companionship, energy management, safety  protection, entertainment experience, and healthy living, promoting the  evolution of smart spaces from single-device control to full-scenario  intelligent services.

 

## **二、赛事任务 | Challenge Task**

设计并实现一套面向家庭应用场景的 AI Agent 解决方案。作品需以智能体为核心架构，围绕真实家庭生活需求，展示其如何理解用户意图、感知空间状态、调用设备能力、进行任务规划，并最终完成主动或半主动的空间服务。

参赛作品可围绕但不限于以下方向展开：

 **1.家庭智能管家 Agent：**根据家庭成员习惯、日程、环境状态和设备状态，提供主动提醒、设备控制、场景推荐及生活服务。

**2.老人关怀与安全守护 Agent：**结合空间感知、行为识别和异常提醒能力，实现跌倒风险提醒、长时间未活动监测、夜间照明联动、紧急通知等功能。

**3.儿童陪伴与学习 Agent：**面向儿童学习、娱乐和陪伴场景，提供语音互动、内容推荐、学习引导、家长管控及安全提醒。

**4.家庭能源管理 Agent：**根据天气、室内环境、设备耗能、家庭使用习惯，智能调节空调、照明、窗帘及其他设备，实现舒适与节能平衡。

**5.家庭娱乐与沉浸体验 Agent：**联动灯光、窗帘、音响、电视、氛围设备等，打造观影、聚会、阅读、睡眠、运动等沉浸式家庭场景。

**6.多成员个性化服务 Agent：**识别不同家庭成员的偏好和习惯，为不同成员提供差异化的空间服务，如温度、灯光、音乐、场景模式等个性化设置。

作品应重点体现 Agent 的核心能力，包括但不限于：多轮交互、意图理解、记忆能力、任务规划、设备调用、场景联动、主动服务、个性化学习及可扩展生态接入。

Based on AI Agent technology, participants are expected to design and  implement an AI Agent solution for home application scenarios. The work  should use the Agent as the core architecture and demonstrate how it  understands user intent, senses spatial status, calls device  capabilities, plans tasks, and finally completes proactive or  semi-proactive space services.

Suggested directions include but are not limited to:

**1.Home AI Butler Agent:** Provides proactive reminders, device control, scenario recommendations, and life services based on family habits, schedules, environmental status, and  device status.

**2.Elderly Care and Safety Protection Agent:** Uses spatial sensing, behavior recognition, and abnormal event alerts to  support fall-risk reminders, long-period inactivity monitoring, night  lighting linkage, and emergency notifications.

**3.Child Companionship and Learning Agent:** Provides voice interaction, content recommendation, learning guidance, parental  control, and safety reminders for children's learning and companionship  scenarios.

**4.Home Energy Management Agent:** Intelligently adjusts air conditioning, lighting, curtains, and other devices based  on weather, indoor environment, energy consumption, and household habits to balance comfort and energy efficiency.

**5.Home Entertainment and Immersive Experience Agent:** Connects lighting, curtains, speakers, TVs, and ambient devices to create  immersive scenarios such as movie watching, parties, reading, sleeping,  and exercise.

**6.Personalized Multi-Member Service Agent:** Identifies preferences and habits of different family members and provides  personalized spatial services such as temperature, lighting, music, and  scene modes.

Works should highlight core Agent capabilities,  including but not limited to multi-turn interaction, intent  understanding, memory, task planning, device invocation, scenario  linkage, proactive service, personalized learning, and scalable  ecosystem integration.

 

## **三、评审规则 | Evaluation Rules**

### **1. 赛道初赛 | Preliminary Round**

**初赛评审形式**：由讯飞开放平台、SpaceMind 相关技术专家、智能空间行业专家及主办方邀请的专业评委，对参赛团队提交的作品方案进行闭门式综合评审。评审将重点关注作品与家庭应用场景的结合度、Agent 架构设计、技术可行性、创新价值及商业应用潜力。

组委会将根据初赛报名情况、作品提交数量及作品质量，综合评选优秀团队进入决赛，并于大赛官网进行名单公示。入围团队将以指定的线上或线下形式进行项目路演及作品展示。

**Preliminary Evaluation Format**: Technical experts from iFLYTEK Open Platform, SpaceMind-related  experts, intelligent space industry experts, and invited judges will  conduct a closed-door comprehensive review of submitted works. The  evaluation will focus on the relevance to home application scenarios,  Agent architecture design, technical feasibility, innovation value, and  commercial application potential.

Based on the number of  submissions, overall participation, and project quality, the organizing  committee will select outstanding teams to enter the final round. The  finalist list will be announced on the official competition website.  Finalist teams will participate in online or offline project roadshows  and demonstrations as designated by the organizing committee.

|     等。     |      |                                                              |

### **2. 赛道决赛 | Final Round**

**决赛评审形式：**

1）参赛团队面向观众与评委，以线上或线下路演形式，结合答辩 PPT、演示视频、产品 DEMO 或实操演示进行项目展示；

2）评委由主办方邀请的 AI 技术专家、智能空间行业专家、投资及产业专家组成；

3）评委根据决赛评分标准进行综合打分，并根据最终得分进行排名；

4）根据分数排名，选出赛道三甲团队；

5）入选团队需配合组委会安排，参与线上或线下路演、展示及传播活动。若主动弃赛或因其他原因未参与路演，组委会有权取消其后续参赛资格并另行增补其他团队。

**Final Evaluation Format:**

1）Participating teams will present their projects to the audience and judges through  online or offline roadshows, supported by pitch decks, demo videos,  product demos, or live demonstrations.

2）Judges will include AI  technical experts, intelligent space industry experts, investment  experts, and industry representatives invited by the organizer.

3）Judges will score each project based on the final evaluation criteria and rank the teams according to their total scores.

4）The top three teams of the track will be selected based on final scores.

5）Selected teams must cooperate with the organizing committee to participate in  online or offline roadshows, showcases, and promotional activities. If a team withdraws or fails to participate, the organizing committee  reserves the right to cancel its qualification and select an alternative team.
